Common questions

Where does the Quran text come from? The verified Uthmani text from the Tanzil Project. Translations are the published works of their translators, credited in Settings → About.

Are the stories tafsir? No. Story Mode offers reflective retellings to help you connect with each surah. For scholarly explanation, please consult a trusted tafsir or scholar.

Prayer times look off. Open Prayer → Calculation and choose the method your local masjid uses, and enable Hanafi Asr if you follow it.

Can I listen offline? Yes. Open any surah and tap the download button.
