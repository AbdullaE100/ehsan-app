Privacy Policy

Ehsan is free, has no ads, and needs no account. What you read, save and pray stays between you and your Lord.

What we collect

Nothing. Ehsan has no servers of its own, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

What stays on your device

  • Your reading progress, bookmarks, memorization progress, streak, settings and finished stories are saved only on your phone.
  • Location. If you allow it, your location is used on your device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. It is rounded to about 1 km and never leaves your phone.
  • Notifications. Prayer reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. No push service is used.

Network requests

  • Recitation audio is streamed from the Islamic Network CDN (cdn.islamic.network) when you press play or download a surah. As with any web request, this reveals your IP address to that service. Ehsan sends nothing else.
  • The Quran text, translations and stories are bundled in the app and work offline.

Children

Ehsan is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here. Questions: open a support request.
